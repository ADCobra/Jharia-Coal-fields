# Fire area against the NRSC coal fire mapping

NRSC (ISRO) mapped the Jharia coalfield fire area as about 3.28 km² in 2017 and about 1.89 km² in 2020, using Landsat-8 band 10 ([BCCL fire report](https://www.bcclweb.in/masterplan/FireDrilling.pdf)). This compares the total area of non-industrial hotspots per clear pass (cloud at most 20 %) with those figures. Thresholds and methods differ, so a ratio between about 0.5 and 2 means the detector is in the right range; it is not a pixel-level validation.
